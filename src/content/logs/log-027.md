Soo I recently joined IIT Delhi as an MS Research student, I'll be working on Autonomous Vehicles, more specifically the VLAs and policies in these Connected Autonomous Vehicles. It's been almost 2 weeks since, I've joined and my feelings are still mixed but lots of things have changed about those too. 

Initially, I hated it a little much, since I've been skeptical about doing masters, doing education itself in India, if I would find myself aligned with the research direction that is not directly robotics (the lab revolves more around V2X of the CAVs rather than policy and controls). 

But at the same time, I've started to enjoy the little life around here. Initially I was feeling a little out of place in a more over-confident and superior than others' way. (Which I still feel, which I wanted to not feel, considering I'm at one of the best institutes in my country) but it still is there which had me worrying and self loathing that I'm not doing enough to be where I deserve to be. 

I know I sound like a douchebag here but it's true, I almost always have felt like a fairly good problem solver (in maths engineering sense if you understand), which is surprisingly not true for a lot of even experienced people. I'm not saying I am more knowledgable person than everyone, that is almost opposite, I feel like new to every new problem, I'm talking about the ability to solve problems, and adapt to new problems itself and you can say aptitude in a way. I used to be bothered by this, a lot, but partly it's the expertise problem, I often have niche expertise so I barely meet people with similar interests that read that much about those topics. And on similar age, I feel most smart people are consumed early by industry (blaming the capitalist society and the academia payoff). I myself made a hard instant money vs future phd decision and well, you know which trajectory felt safer for my ambitions.


But I guess those feelings go away once you start working on problems and make some friends. 
The more fun parts are, I'm taking two courses this semester, REINFORCEMENT LEARNING and Computer Vision, I'm having lots of fun with those. I can read the books all day honestly, and still feel like reading more. I mean I wanted to read Sutton and barto since last year but procastinating since so long. And we're recommended Forsyth and Ponce (Computer Vision: A modern approach) by the CV professor. 

RL is surprisingly still challenging in some ways to me, since I'm a novice programmer at best. And for this  some of the cool history of Dynamic Programming and ideas around there are often used. Soo, I'm considering reading some more DP  (the  RL professor has added reading source about Neuro Dynamic Programming so maybe that). Apart from that, I still feel I have massive upper hand in RL than most people lol. In CV I don't have much experience tbh but still I'm great at maths so it's not much of an issue and I'm already familiar with CV. So not much of an issue. 

At the same time I've been tasked with 1-2 research papers with deadline on September 1st soo you know life's hectic lol. But I feel papers aren't that hard to come, GPUs are. And I don't have one yet.
